USA Explained Publisher

A private publishing tool for the USA Explained YouTube channel

What is USA Explained?

USA Explained is a YouTube channel about everyday American life: why things in America work the way they do, explained with maps, real numbers and short, clear stories. Non-political.

What does this tool do?

USA Explained Publisher is used only by the owner of the USA Explained channel, for that channel only. It:

  • uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube and schedules them,
  • sets their titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and playlists,
  • posts the channel's own comments under its own videos,
  • reads the channel's own view and audience retention statistics.

It is not offered to other creators and does not access anyone else's account.
